A little closer to wild.

Feather is an interactive celebration of the birds and landscapes of Northern Colorado. Choose a bird to explore its field note, then drag the scene to look around.

The birds are original, stylized 3D interpretations rather than identification guides. Lengths are approximate adult ranges; size comparisons use the midpoint of each range. Some broad names in the original list are represented by a familiar species.
